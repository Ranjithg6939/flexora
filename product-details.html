Recommended Applications

  • Outdoor Highway Hoardings & Billboards
  • Retail Storefront Sale & Promotional Banners
  • Building Scaffolding Wraps & Real Estate Site Signs
  • Trade Show Backdrops & Event Stage Wall Facings
Brass Eyelets / Grommets

Rust-proof solid brass eyelets machine-stamped through double-folded hems every 2 feet or at 4 corners for fast rope/zip-tie rigging.

Heat-Sealed Welded Hems

1.5-inch continuous perimeter heat weld preventing raw thread fraying and drastically enhancing wind resistance.

Pole Pockets (Top & Bottom)

2-inch to 4-inch sleeve loops stitched or heat-welded along edges to accommodate aluminum pipes, rods, or scaffolding bars.

Our industrial Eco-Solvent and UV curable inks are rated for 2 to 3 years of direct outdoor exposure without visible color fading or brittleness. Star Flex and 440 GSM Blackout materials can last even longer.

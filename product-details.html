Heat-Sealed Welded Hems

1.5-inch continuous perimeter heat weld preventing raw thread fraying and drastically enhancing wind resistance.

2-inch to 4-inch sleeve loops stitched or heat-welded along edges to accommodate aluminum pipes, rods, or scaffolding bars.

Our Story & Heritage

Built on Precision, Heavy-Duty Machinery & Speed

Founded in 2012, FLEXORA began with a single wide-format solvent printer and an uncompromising vision: eliminate blurry, washed-out outdoor hoardings and deliver vivid, commercial-grade flex prints at true factory-direct wholesale pricing.

Today, our 25,000 sq.ft temperature-controlled industrial print facility houses over 14 wide-format and UV flatbed printing machines. We process over 45,000 square feet of flex, vinyl, and rigid board substrates every day for Fortune 500 brands, advertising agencies, and local enterprises alike.

Our Mission

To empower marketing teams, brands, and retail networks with unmatched large-format print fidelity, rigorous structural eyeletting and welding, and lightning-fast turnaround times that keep marketing campaigns right on schedule.

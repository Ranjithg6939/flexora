Our Story & Heritage

Built on Precision, Heavy-Duty Machinery & Speed

Founded in 2012, FLEXORA began with a single wide-format solvent printer and an uncompromising vision: eliminate blurry, washed-out outdoor hoardings and deliver vivid, commercial-grade flex prints at true factory-direct wholesale pricing.

Today, our 25,000 sq.ft temperature-controlled industrial print facility houses over 14 wide-format and UV flatbed printing machines. We process over 45,000 square feet of flex, vinyl, and rigid board substrates every day for Fortune 500 brands, advertising agencies, and local enterprises alike.

Our Mission

To empower marketing teams, brands, and retail networks with unmatched large-format print fidelity, rigorous structural eyeletting and welding, and lightning-fast turnaround times that keep marketing campaigns right on schedule.

Our Vision

To be the premier industrial print hub recognized nationwide for adopting zero-VOC UV curable inks, automated finishing robotics, and sustainable flex media alternatives without sacrificing outdoor longevity or color depth.

Plant Infrastructure

Industrial Press Floor Capabilities

Our plant combines cutting-edge high-frequency heat welders, automated grommet stations, and wide-format roll-to-roll printers.

SuperWide Roll-to-Roll

Up to 5.0 meters (16.4 ft) seamless width printing for immense stadium banners and highway billboards.

Rigid UV Flatbed

Direct printing onto 8x4 ft Sunboard, acrylic, corrugated plastic, and aluminum composite panels.

RF Seam Welding

Radio frequency heat sealing generates waterproof, wind-resistant bonded seams that outlast stitched threads.

CNC Precision Routing

Automated contour shape cutting for standees, acrylic 3D letters, and custom retail POS displays.

Wind-Slit Aerodynamics

Engineered wind relief perforations and reinforced perimeter webbings for rooftop and windy installations.

UV Liquid Lamination

Optional anti-graffiti and chemical-resistant topcoats protecting vehicle wraps from harsh weather and road salts.

Company Milestones

Shop Inception

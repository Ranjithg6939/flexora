Commercial agreements governing artwork proofing, color tolerance, and courier delivery.

1. Artwork Approval & Soft Proofing

Clients are responsible for proofreading all typography, spelling, and design elements prior to final press authorization. FLEXORA is not liable for typographical errors present in customer-submitted vector or raster files.

2. Color Matching & Pantone Tolerances

Digital flex and banner printing utilizes CMYK ink processes. A commercial industry tolerance of ±5% Delta-E is standard across solvent and UV inks depending on the substrate's base whiteness.

3. Production Deadlines & Dispatch

Production lead times commence once print-ready artwork is approved and payment or purchase order is confirmed. FLEXORA guarantees handover to logistics partners within stated turnaround windows.
